Activity Registration: Bound

Every athlete registers for wrestling in Bound each year. It's also where you upload a physical and sign the consent forms.

  1. Open BoundUse the button below or scan the code, then click the Registration tab.
  2. Log in or create an account
    Returning studentsClick your student portal and register for activities. Skip step 3.
    New to BoundClick "Create Your Account Now" to make a family account, then go to step 3.
  3. Add your studentClick "Add a Student" and search for your student. Verify the information and click "Connect to My Student".
